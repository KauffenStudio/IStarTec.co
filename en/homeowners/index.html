Does iStarTec work with homeowners in the Algarve?

Yes. iStarTec installs solar thermal and underfloor heating and replumbs private houses across the Algarve, including in occupied homes. This is renovation and system replacement work — the company does not do one-off repairs, call-outs, maintenance contracts or drain clearing.

  • Solar thermal (hot water): Bosch systems, sized to the household's demand
  • Underfloor heating: Uponor PEX pipe, with a 10-year warranty
  • Plumbing renovation: cold water, hot water, waste and manifolds
  • Swimming pools: pool plumbing, plant room and salt treatment with automatic pH
  • Quote: free, after a site visit, answered within 24 working hours
  • What we do not do: one-off repairs, service contracts, drain clearing and natural gas
  • Area: the whole Algarve, from the office in Albufeira
  • Experience: founded in 2000, IMPIC licence category 4 class 3

How we work with you

A clear process, with no surprises. The site visit and quote are free — you only decide to go ahead once you know exactly what you will pay and how long it takes.

1

Contact and site visit

A call or email to tell us what you have in mind. We come to your house, measure up and assess what is feasible. Free and with no obligation.

2

Detailed quote

Within 5 working days you get a written quote with materials, labour, timescale and validity. No hidden costs.

3

Installation

A team of 2 to 4 technicians, at the agreed hours. In an occupied home we always leave water and power available and clean up at the end of each day.

4

Warranty and support

1 year of iStarTec warranty on workmanship and 2 years on materials. Equipment carries additional manufacturer warranties (Bosch, Uponor). Documentation handed over in writing.

What we do not do — to be straight with you

We do not do small repairs (a dripping tap, a broken cistern, blocked drains). For those, we recommend a local repair plumber — it will cost you less than sending our team out.

We do not do domestic service contracts or 24-hour call-outs. We concentrate on full renovations, where our technical experience makes a real difference.

We do not do natural gas installations or gas certifications. We recommend specialists for that part.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work directly with homeowners, or only with construction companies?

Both. Our 25+ year track record is in residential construction for developers, but we now also take on work for homeowners — provided it is a full renovation or a system replacement (solar, underfloor heating, the whole house replumbed). We do not do small repairs or one-off call-outs.

Do you work in occupied homes?

Yes. Most private clients want the system replaced without moving out. We phase the work to keep disruption down: we arrive early, protect the space, leave water and power available at the end of every day, and clean the work area before we go.

How long does an installation take?

It depends on the service: solar thermal typically 2 to 3 days; underfloor heating in a villa between 1 and 2 weeks (with floor works); a full plumbing renovation 2 to 4 weeks. We always give you a detailed schedule before starting.

What warranty do you offer?

iStarTec warranty: 1 year on installation workmanship and 2 years on materials. Manufacturers add their own — Bosch up to 10 years on some collectors, Uponor 10 years on PEX pipe. You always get copies of the warranties in writing when the job is finished.

Do you do small repairs or one-off call-outs?

No. We focus on full renovations and system installations — we do not do domestic service contracts or 24-hour call-outs. For a single leak or breakdown, we recommend a local repair plumber.

Are there tax incentives for solar thermal?

Yes. A solar thermal installation for domestic hot water can qualify for the PRR / Environmental Fund energy efficiency programme and for the Portuguese income tax deduction on renewable energy equipment. We supply all the documentation you need to apply.
