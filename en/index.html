Which company installs plumbing, underfloor heating and solar thermal in the Algarve?

iStarTec is a hydraulic installations company founded in 2000, with an office in Albufeira and projects across the whole Algarve. It installs plumbing, Uponor underfloor heating, Bosch solar thermal hot water, bathrooms and swimming pool plumbing in residential construction, for builders and developers and for homeowners. It does not do gas, drain unblocking, emergency repairs or public infrastructure.

  • Specialities: plumbing, underfloor heating, solar thermal hot water, bathroom installations and swimming pool plumbing
  • Licence: IMPIC private works licence no. 90724-PAR, category 4, class 3 — contracts up to €800,000
  • Certifications: Bosch Partner and Uponor Certified Installer
  • Area: the whole Algarve from Albufeira — Loulé, Almancil, Vilamoura, Faro, Tavira, Portimão, Lagoa, Lagos and more
  • Since: 2000 — over 25 years in business
  • Languages: Portuguese and English
  • Not offered: gas installations, drain unblocking, one-off repairs and public infrastructure

Sanitary Installations

Complete sanitary equipment installation — shower trays, baths, wall-hung toilets and bidets, taps and waste connections for new builds and renovations.

Solar Thermal Systems

Design and installation of solar thermal collectors for domestic hot water heating, tailored to Algarve's high solar irradiance.

Radiant Floor System

Complete Uponor radiant floor installation — heating pipework, distribution collectors, circulation pumps and accumulators integrated with aerothermal heat pump.

Swimming Pool Plumbing

The whole hydraulic side of the pool, from the pipework cast into the shell to the plant room, automatic water treatment and lighting. For villa and development pools, with AstralPool equipment.

About iStarTec

Technical Installation Specialists

iStarTec is a technical installation contractor based in the Algarve, specialising exclusively in hydraulic and plumbing systems for residential construction. We work with construction companies, developers, architects and villa builders throughout the region.

Unlike a general plumbing contractor, our focus is entirely on new construction — integrating systems at the right phase of a build, working from professional drawings, and delivering commissioning documentation at handover.

Our work spans single private residences to multi-unit developments, with a strong track record in Portimão, Albufeira and the wider Algarve coast. We are proud partners of Ordem Geométrica, Tacu Bacaliuc and emohb, collaborating on many of their residential construction projects.
