Plumbing & Hydraulic Installations in Faro

Hydraulic installations for residential construction in Faro and the eastern Algarve (Sotavento). Integrated plumbing, solar thermal and heat pump systems for developers and builders.

Who installs plumbing, underfloor heating and solar thermal in Faro?

iStarTec is a hydraulic installations company founded in 2000 that works in Faro, about 40 km from its office in Albufeira. It installs plumbing (hot and cold water, wastewater and rainwater drainage), Uponor hydraulic underfloor heating, Bosch solar thermal hot water and bathrooms, in new builds and renovations: residential construction for developers and builders, with integrated hot water and heat pump systems. It does not do gas, drain unblocking or emergency repairs.

iStarTec in Faro

Faro is the capital of the Algarve, and demand here differs from the western Algarve (Barlavento): a wider mix of building types (apartments, villas, mixed-use buildings) and a steadier pace of construction throughout the year. Our installations in Faro adapt to that variety, from multi-unit residential buildings to private homes, with systems that meet the energy-efficiency regulations, which are becoming ever stricter for new builds.

Our services in Faro

Complete hydraulic installations matched to the technical profile of each project in Faro — plumbing, hot water, solar thermal systems and heat pumps.

Plumbing Installations

Water supply networks (multilayer, PEX, copper), wastewater systems, internal rainwater drainage and ventilation for residential buildings and villas in Faro.

Solar Thermal Systems

Bosch solar thermal collectors for hot water, sized for the Algarve's high solar irradiance, with the primary circuit in copper. Compliant with energy-efficiency regulations.

Underfloor Heating

Hydraulic underfloor heating with Uponor PEX pipe, zone manifolds and thermostatic control, paired with an air-source heat pump.

Bathroom Installations

Supply and installation of premium sanitaryware for bathrooms in villas and developments in Faro.

Building or renovating your own home in Faro?

We also work directly for homeowners in Faro, Montenegro, Gambelas, Estoi and Santa Bárbara de Nexe — including in homes that are lived in during the works. This is installation and system replacement, not repair work. A technical visit and quote are free, and we reply within 24 working hours.

Frequently Asked Questions — Faro

Do you work in Faro?

Yes. Faro is within our service area, along with the whole of the Algarve. We cover everything from new builds to renovations, in private homes and residential developments.

How quickly do you respond to a request in Faro?

We reply to quote requests within 24 working hours. For jobs in Faro, we carry out a site visit before proposing a final solution.

Do you work with engineering firms in Faro?

Yes. We work regularly with engineering firms at the design stage — hydraulic calculations, sizing of solar thermal systems and heat pumps, and drawing up specifications.
