iStarTec in Vila do Bispo

Vila do Bispo is the most exposed corner of the Algarve. In Sagres the wind blows almost every day and salt air reaches everything outside. The area lives largely on tourism: holiday rentals, surf houses, small hotels and holiday villas in Salema and Budens. Inland, in Vila do Bispo, Raposeira and Barão de São Miguel, there are old houses being restored and new villas for people who live here all year.

In a rental with many guests, the usual problem is hot water running out when everyone comes back from the beach at once. That is solved at design stage, with the cylinder and heating output sized for peak demand and a hot water return so nobody waits at the tap. Outside, collectors, pipes and fixings have to stand up to wind and salt.

Our services in Vila do Bispo

In Vila do Bispo we carry out complete installations in villas and restored houses, and hot water systems for holiday rentals and tourism.

Plumbing

Hot and cold water in multilayer or PEX and internal drainage in PVC, in new builds and when replacing old pipework. Outdoor pipes protected from sun and salt air.

Hot water for holiday rentals

Bosch solar thermal with a copper primary circuit, a heat pump and a cylinder sized for many showers in a row. A hot water return so hot water reaches the tap without waste.

Underfloor heating

Uponor hydraulic underfloor heating in new builds and renovations. In houses exposed to the Sagres wind it gives steady heat through the whole house and runs on a heat pump.

Bathrooms and pools

Fitting of sanitaryware and mixers in new and renovated bathrooms. Pool pipework, plant room and AstralPool equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions — Vila do Bispo

Do you work in Sagres and Vila do Bispo?

Yes. iStarTec works across the whole Algarve. In the municipality of Vila do Bispo we take on jobs in Sagres, Vila do Bispo, Budens, Salema, Raposeira, Barão de São Miguel and Figueira, for homeowners, holiday rentals and builders.

My rental runs out of hot water when it is full. Can that be fixed?

Usually, yes. We start by working out how many showers in a row the house has to handle and compare that with the current cylinder volume and heating output. Then we propose the right cylinder, heat pump or solar thermal system, plus a hot water return if the bathrooms are far from the cylinder.

Can solar collectors handle the wind in Sagres?

Yes, as long as the frame and fixings are chosen and installed for that exposure. We decide the collector position and fixing type case by case, based on the roof and its orientation.

Can I replace my gas water heater with a heat pump?

Yes. We install the heat pump and connect the water side. The gas point is decommissioned by a certified gas company, because iStarTec does not work on gas.
