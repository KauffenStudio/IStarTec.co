Careers

Work With Us

We are looking for plumbers, plumber's mates and hydraulic installation technicians for villas, apartment buildings and tourism developments across the Algarve. The application takes about 3 minutes and a CV is optional.

Fill in the application

The work

  • Where

    Sites across the Algarve, from Lagos to Tavira.

  • What we do

    Hot and cold water pipework in multilayer and PPR, drainage, underfloor heating, solar thermal, heat pumps, pool plumbing and fitting sanitary ware. We do not work on gas.

  • Who we are looking for

    Qualified and semi-qualified plumbers, plumber's mates, and hot water and solar technicians. Tell us the experience you actually have: that is what we assess.

How it works

  1. You send your application with this form.
  2. We read the experience you describe.
  3. If your profile matches what we need, we call you to arrange a conversation.

Prefer email? Send your CV to geral@istartec.co with the subject “Application”.
