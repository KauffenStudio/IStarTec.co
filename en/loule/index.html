Frequently Asked Questions — Loulé

What kind of projects do you take on around Loulé, Almancil and Vilamoura?

We work with builders and developers on high-end detached villas (4 to 6 bedrooms), tourist-residential villas and resort developments, and directly with homeowners on underfloor heating, solar thermal hot water and whole-house replumbing. The end client in this part of the Algarve expects high technical standards, good materials and firm deadlines, and that is where most of our track record in the area comes from.

Do you install swimming pool heating?

Yes. We install pool heating with an air-source heat pump, with solar thermal as back-up where it makes sense, and we coordinate the pool plumbing with the villa's hot water and underfloor heating so the systems work together efficiently.

How long does a full installation take on a premium villa in Almancil?

A premium villa in Almancil, Vale do Lobo or Quinta do Lago typically takes 10 to 16 weeks for the complete hydraulic installation: internal plumbing, reversible underfloor heating in every room, solar plus heat pump for hot water, premium bathrooms and pool or spa connections. The schedule is coordinated with the construction phases.

Do you work on condominium projects in Vilamoura?

Yes. We carry out hydraulic installations in condominiums and resort developments in Vilamoura, following the specifications typical of the area (water treatment, resort-approved materials, coordination with the condominium management).

Do you do emergency plumbing repairs in Loulé?

No. iStarTec installs and replaces complete systems; it does not do one-off repairs, emergency call-outs, maintenance contracts or drain unblocking. For a single leak or breakdown, a local repair plumber is the right call.
