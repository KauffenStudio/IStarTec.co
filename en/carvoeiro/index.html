iStarTec in Carvoeiro

Carvoeiro and Ferragudo are largely second-home country. There are clifftop villas, holiday resorts with shared pools, apartments to let and many British, Irish and Dutch owners who spend part of the year here. Many of these homes were built from the 1980s onwards and are now being renovated.

A house used for only part of the year has its own problems: water standing in the cylinder for months, seals drying out, systems nobody keeps an eye on. In a renovation we redo the pipework, choose a hot water system that heats when the house is occupied and runs a hygiene cycle when it is used again, and fit accessible isolation valves so the house can be shut down safely.

Our services in Carvoeiro

In Carvoeiro and Ferragudo most of our work is renovating villas and apartments, hot water systems and pool plumbing.

Replumbing

Old pipework replaced with multilayer or PEX, with accessible manifolds and zone isolation valves, and new internal drainage in PVC.

Hot water for holiday homes

Bosch heat pumps and solar thermal with a copper primary circuit, with cylinder and scheduling chosen for seasonal use and water hygiene.

Underfloor heating

Uponor hydraulic underfloor heating in renovations, where floor height allows, and in new builds, run from a heat pump.

Pools

Plant room, saltwater chlorination, LED, automatic top-up and pool heat pump, with AstralPool equipment, for villas and resorts.

Frequently Asked Questions — Carvoeiro

Do you work in Carvoeiro and Ferragudo?

Yes. iStarTec works across the whole Algarve. In the municipality of Lagoa we take on jobs in Carvoeiro, Ferragudo, Benagil, Porches and Lagoa town, for owners, resorts and builders.

I live outside Portugal. Can the work be done without me here?

Yes. We do the technical visit with whoever holds the keys, send the quote by email in English or Portuguese, and keep you updated on progress by email or WhatsApp. Start and handover dates are agreed with you.

What hot water system makes sense in a holiday home?

One that heats only when the house is occupied and runs a hygiene cycle when it is used again. Scheduled heat pumps and solar thermal with back-up are the most common options; the cylinder size follows how many people stay.

Can you do just the pool, without touching the house?

Yes. We replace or install the plant room, saltwater treatment, lighting and pool heat pump, with AstralPool equipment. We also supply equipment only.
