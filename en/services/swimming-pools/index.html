Swimming Pool Plumbing in the Algarve

The whole hydraulic side of your pool, from the pipework cast into the shell to the plant room, automatic water treatment, lighting and heating. AstralPool equipment, for contractors, developers and villa owners.

What does swimming pool plumbing include?

It covers the fittings embedded in the pool shell (skimmers, main drain, return inlets and vacuum points), the pipework to the plant room, the pump and filter, automatic water treatment, lighting and commissioning. iStarTec installs all of this for villa and development pools across the Algarve, using AstralPool equipment. Building the pool itself is the builder's job.

  • Embedded pipework: PN10 PVC pressure pipe, laid before the concrete pour
  • Plant room: pump, sand or activated-glass filter, 6-way multiport valve
  • Treatment: salt electrolysis with automatic pH control
  • Lighting: RGB LED pool lights and a dedicated pool control panel
  • Heating: pool heat pump, as an option
  • Equipment: AstralPool
  • Not included: excavation, concrete shell, waterproofing and tiling

What we install

A pool's hydraulics go in at two different points in the build. The first part ends up hidden in the concrete: the fittings the water leaves and returns through, and the pipes that connect them to the plant room. The second is the plant room itself, where the water is pumped, filtered, treated and, if you want it, heated. We do both, to the same standard as the water systems we install in the villas themselves.

  • Skimmers, anti-vortex main drain, adjustable return inlets, vacuum points and light niches
  • PN10 PVC pressure pipework (suction, return and delivery), with isolation valves on each circuit
  • Pump with strainer basket, fixed or variable speed
  • Sand or activated-glass filter with 6-way multiport valve, manifolds and check valves
  • Salt electrolysis with automatic pH control (probe and dosing pump)
  • Underwater RGB LED lights and a control panel with 12 V transformer, RCD and filtration timer
  • Level controller for automatic top-up
  • Pool heat pump (optional)
  • Commissioning: filter backwash, purging the circuit, calibrating pH and salt, programming, and a walkthrough for the owner

How the water circulates

In a properly built circuit the water always follows the same path, and the order of the equipment matters:

  1. Suction through the skimmers (surface water) and the main drain (bottom layer).
  2. Strainer and pump: the basket catches leaves and debris before they reach the pump.
  3. Filter: sand or activated glass holds back the fine particles.
  4. Heat pump, if fitted, warms the already-filtered water.
  5. Salt cell: last in line, so the chlorine it produces never passes through the heat pump's exchanger.
  6. Return to the pool through inlets positioned to push surface water towards the skimmers.

Pump and filter sized to the pool

A residential pool should turn over its full volume every 4 to 6 hours. We start from the volume, taken from the plans and sections, and choose the pump and filter as a pair. A real example, from a 12.50 × 5.50 m pool we quoted in Albufeira:

Pool volume, at an average depth of 1.20 m.

≈ 20 m³/h

≈ 4 hours

Ø800 filter

The balance is what matters. A bigger pump on the same filter pushes water through the sand too fast and filters worse. A bigger filter on the same pump costs more and gains nothing.

Salt treatment with automatic pH

With salt electrolysis, salt is dissolved in the water (about 5 g/L, a tenth of sea water) and a cell turns it into chlorine as the water circulates. Once it has disinfected, the chlorine turns back into salt, so consumption is low and you no longer buy, carry or store chlorine. At the same time a probe reads the pH continuously and a dosing pump keeps it between 7.0 and 7.4. In the Algarve this matters: hard water pushes the pH up, and at high pH chlorine loses its effect.

Less upkeep

No tablets or granules. You only top up the salt lost in filter backwashes.

Gentler water

Fewer chloramines, the compounds behind the chlorine smell and stinging eyes.

pH under control

Protects the finish, seals and equipment, and prevents cloudy water and scale.

When we come on site

Stage 1: before the pour

We place the embedded fittings and pipework before the pool walls and floor slab are poured. We need to be called at that point, because afterwards there is no clean way to do it.

Stage 2: plant room

Once the pool is finished, we fit the pump, filter, treatment, control panel and heat pump if there is one, then commission the system.

Timing

For a villa pool, around 6 to 8 working days on site, split across the two stages and fitted to the build programme.

Where our work starts and stops

So there is no confusion between trades, here is what we do not do:

  • Pool construction: excavation, concrete shell, waterproofing, tiling and coping
  • Building the plant room, including its ventilation and drainage
  • Power supply up to the pool control panel and earthing, which belong to the site electrician. From the panel onwards, the work is ours.

We also supply equipment only (filtration, treatment, heating and control panels), already sized for the pool, when another team is doing the installation.

Frequently Asked Questions — Pools

Does iStarTec build swimming pools?

No. We do the hydraulic side and the equipment: embedded fittings, pipework, plant room, water treatment, lighting and heating. Excavation, the concrete shell, waterproofing and tiling belong to the builder, and we coordinate with them from the start.

At what stage should iStarTec be on site?

Before the walls and floor slab of the pool are poured. That is when the skimmers, main drain, return inlets and the pipework that sits inside the concrete go in. We come back later to fit out the plant room, connect the equipment and commission the pool.

Chlorine or salt: which treatment is better?

For most villa pools we recommend salt electrolysis with automatic pH control. The salt (about 5 g/L) is turned into chlorine inside the system, so there is no chlorine to buy or dose, and the pH probe corrects the water continuously. This matters in the Algarve: hard water pushes the pH up, and at high pH chlorine works poorly.

Can you heat the pool?

Yes, as an option. We install a pool heat pump sized to the pool volume and the months you want to swim, connected to the filtration circuit ahead of the salt cell.

Do you supply pool equipment only?

Yes. Where another team does the installation, we supply AstralPool filtration, treatment, heating and control equipment, already sized for the pool volume.

How much does pool plumbing cost?

It depends on the pool volume, the number of embedded fittings, the distance to the plant room and the treatment and heating you choose. We quote from the pool drawings or plan, free of charge, within 24 working hours.
