iStarTec in Silves

Silves is a municipality of two territories. To the north lies the hilly interior — detached houses and country homes, where winters are considerably colder than on the coast and sun exposure is excellent all year round. To the south are Armação de Pêra and Pêra, with developments and seafront apartments, where the internal pipework has to cope with salt air and very concentrated seasonal occupancy. They are two distinct types of job, served by the same team.

Our services in Silves

We work on new builds and renovations. A good part of the housing in Silves is forty years old or more, with galvanised internal pipework whose bore has narrowed with scale — weak pressure and recurring leaks. We replace that pipework with multilayer or PEX with minimal wall chasing, keeping the house habitable for most of the works.

Plumbing Installations

Internal hot and cold water networks in multilayer, PEX or copper, with accessible manifolds, and internal wastewater and rainwater drainage in PVC-U. For detached houses inland and for developments on the coast.

Solar Thermal Systems

Bosch solar thermal for hot water, with the primary circuit in copper. Silves has excellent sun exposure and little shading inland — ideal conditions for a system that covers most of a family's hot water over the year.

Underfloor Heating

Uponor PEX underfloor heating. In the country houses inland, where winter nights get considerably colder than on the coast, it is the solution that gives the most comfort for every euro spent on heating.

Bathroom Installations

Supply and fitting of sanitaryware and mixer taps, from standard to premium ranges, for private houses and for accommodation units in Armação de Pêra.

Frequently Asked Questions — Silves

Do you cover both inland Silves and the coast with the same team?

Yes. The whole municipality of Silves is served from our office in Albufeira, about 20 to 35 minutes away depending on the locality. There is no difference in response time between Armação de Pêra, Silves itself and São Bartolomeu de Messines.

Do you work on old buildings in the centre of Silves?

Yes. In old masonry buildings we replace the internal pipework with as little wall chasing as possible and, wherever we can, run the pipes through suspended ceilings or service ducts so that structural walls are not compromised. The pipe routing is agreed with the designer before work starts.

What is the difference between a job inland and one on the coast?

The materials are the same; what changes is the coordination. On a detached house inland we work directly with the owner or with the builder. In an apartment in Armação de Pêra, access and working hours have to be arranged with the condominium management, which usually makes the schedule longer.

Do you install solar thermal and underfloor heating in houses in Silves?

Yes, they are two of our main services. The inland part of the municipality gets excellent sun exposure, which favours solar thermal for hot water, and its winters are colder than on the coast, so low-temperature underfloor heating gives the most comfort for every euro spent on heating.

Do you do emergency plumbing repairs in Silves?

No. iStarTec installs and replaces complete systems; it does not do one-off repairs, emergency call-outs, maintenance contracts or drain unblocking. For a single leak or breakdown, a local repair plumber is the right call.
