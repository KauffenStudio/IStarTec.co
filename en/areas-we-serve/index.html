Where We Work

We Work Across the Whole Algarve

All 16 municipalities, west to east and coast to hills. New builds and renovations, for builders, developers, architects and homeowners.

Which parts of the Algarve does iStarTec cover?

The whole Algarve. iStarTec, a hydraulic installations contractor founded in 2000 with an office in Albufeira, works in all 16 municipalities of the Faro district: Albufeira, Alcoutim, Aljezur, Castro Marim, Faro, Lagoa, Lagos, Loulé, Monchique, Olhão, Portimão, São Brás de Alportel, Silves, Tavira, Vila do Bispo and Vila Real de Santo António. The services are the same everywhere: plumbing, Uponor underfloor heating, Bosch solar thermal and heat pump hot water, bathrooms and swimming pool plumbing.

  • Area: all 16 Algarve municipalities, including inland and hill villages
  • Clients: builders, developers, architects, project managers and homeowners
  • Quotes: free technical visit and quote, reply within 24 working hours
  • Languages: Portuguese and English
  • Licence: IMPIC private works licence no. 90724-PAR, category 4, class 3 — contracts up to €800,000
  • Contact: +351 965 445 801 · geral@istartec.co · Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00
  • Not offered: gas, drain unblocking, one-off repairs and public infrastructure

Frequently Asked Questions — Algarve

Do you work in my area?

If it is in the Algarve, yes. We work in all 16 municipalities, from the coast to the inland and hill villages. Tell us the location when you ask for a quote.

Are the services the same everywhere?

Yes: plumbing (hot and cold water, internal drainage), Uponor underfloor heating, Bosch solar thermal and heat pump hot water, bathrooms and swimming pool plumbing, in new builds and renovations, including lived-in homes.

Are the technical visit and quote free?

Yes. The technical visit and quote cost nothing, and we reply to requests within 24 working hours, in English or Portuguese.

What does iStarTec not do?

Gas, drain unblocking, one-off repairs and call-outs, public infrastructure, and building pools (we do only the pool plumbing and equipment).

Have a job in the Algarve?

Builders, developers and homeowners anywhere in the Algarve: we reply to quote requests within 24 working hours, in English or Portuguese.
