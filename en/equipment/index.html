Bosch solar thermal

Thermosiphon and forced circulation

Bosch

F2/300 L thermosiphon kit

Two collectors and the tank on the roof. Heated water rises into the tank on its own, with no pump and no electricity.

  • 2 FCC220-2V collectors
  • 300 L tank
  • Aluminium (Premium) or galvanised steel (Essence) frame
Bosch

F1/150 and 200 L thermosiphon kit

The single-collector version, for smaller homes or lower hot water use.

  • 1 FCC220-2V collector (FKC on the 200 L)
  • 150 or 200 L tank
  • Flat (F) or pitched (A) roof
Bosch

FCC220-2V flat-plate collector

For forced-circulation systems with the tank indoors. We also fit the FKC-2S and FT 226-2V ranges.

  • Portrait mounting
  • Flat or pitched roof
  • Primary circuit always in copper
Bosch

AGS10-2 station and B-Sol controller

The pump station for the solar loop. The controller only runs the pump when the collectors are hotter than the tank.

  • AGS10-2 for up to 10 collectors
  • AGS20-2 and AGS50-2 for larger systems
  • B-Sol 050, B-Sol 100 and CS 200 controllers

Bosch heat pumps

Heating, cooling and hot water

Bosch

Compress 2000 AWF · 4 to 16 kW

Monobloc: everything in one outdoor unit, with no indoor unit. The simplest choice for villas.

  • R32 refrigerant · A+ to A+++ class
  • Flow temperature up to 65 °C
  • Expansion vessel and pump built in
Bosch

Compress 2000 AWF · 18 to 30 kW

The same range, for large villas and small developments.

  • Two fans · three phase
  • Up to 6 units in cascade (180 kW)
  • Flow temperature up to 60 °C
Bosch

Compress 5800i AW · 4 to 12 kW

Bosch's top range, with a natural refrigerant. Bosch describes it as its quietest heat pump.

  • Natural R290 refrigerant
  • Flow temperature up to 75 °C
  • 28.5 dB(A) at 3 m in night mode (5 kW)
Bosch

Indoor tower module (5800i AW)

Puts the hot water cylinder, pump and 3-way valve in a single cabinet that can stand in a kitchen or utility room.

  • Built-in 180 L hot water cylinder
  • HMI 800 touch screen
  • Takes 0.36 m² of floor
Bosch

Wall-hung indoor module (5800i AW)

For when the hot water cylinder stands separately, sized to the house.

  • 3, 6 or 9 kW back-up heater
  • Choice of cylinder, up to 500 L
  • K40 Wi-Fi and HomeCom Easy app

Bosch hot water cylinders

For solar and heat pumps

Bosch

Stora WS · 310 to 1000 L

Twin-coil cylinder: the lower coil takes the solar heat, the upper coil the heat pump back-up.

  • Vitreous enamel lining
  • 310, 400, 500, 750 and 1000 L
  • Optional 2 to 9 kW immersion heater
Bosch

B-Sol 100 solar controller

Compares the collector and tank temperatures and decides when the solar pump runs.

  • Display showing the system layout
  • Also built into the AGS10 B-Sol 100 station

Uponor underfloor heating

Pipe, board, manifold and controls

Uponor

Comfort Pipe PLUS

PEX-a pipe with an oxygen barrier. Each loop is one continuous length from the manifold, with no joints under the floor.

  • Flexible PEX-a
  • Barrier against oxygen ingress
Uponor

Klett insulation board

The surface works like hook-and-loop: the pipe grips the board without clips and the insulation sends the heat upwards.

  • Printed grid for pipe spacing
  • Quick, clean installation
Uponor

Vario manifold

Feeds the loops in every room. The flow meters keep each loop balanced.

  • Flow meter on every loop
  • Valves ready for actuators
Uponor

Actuator and thermostat

The actuator opens and closes each room's loop when the thermostat calls for heating or cooling.

  • One actuator per loop
  • A thermostat in every room

Plumbing

Cold water, hot water and waste

iStarTec

PEX-AL-PEX multilayer

Aluminium between two layers of PEX: it does not rust, has a smooth inner wall and keeps its shape once bent.

  • Ø16, 20 and 25 mm
  • Manifold per bathroom, isolation valve per fitting
  • Insulated hot water pipe

AstralPool pool equipment

Plant room, treatment and heating

AstralPool

Eco Elyo heat pump

Heats the pool water with energy from the air and stretches the swimming season into spring and autumn.

  • Fitted next to the plant room
  • Connected to the filtration loop
